# POV Statement Activity

Your POV Statement is the foundation of your entire Define Phase. It crystallizes your self-knowledge into a single, honest, actionable articulation of your academic direction. This is not a formal mission statement — it is a working tool. It will evolve. But you need a starting point, and this activity helps you build one.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your Module 2 Work (10–15 minutes)

Open your previous assignments and read through your key insights from:

- Your SWOT Matrix (especially Strengths and Opportunities)
- Empathy Map

As you read, highlight or note the three to five themes that appear most consistently. You are looking for patterns — things that showed up in multiple places.

**Themes I noticed:**
1. Cybersecurity Career Development  
2. Persistence & willingness to learn 
3. Building professional connections & networking  
4. Time management & procrastination 
5. Gaining hands-on cybersecurity experience & confidence 

---

## Step 2 — Answer the POV Builder Questions

**1. Who am I in this moment, academically and personally?**
*(Consider: your background, your identity, your situation as a college student)*
I am currently an online college student pursuing my Bachelors degree in Applied Cybersecurity & Minor in Artificial Intelligence. I am motivated to continue building my knowledge in cybersecurity & technology while gaining the skills & experience needed to prepare myself for my future.  

**2. What do I genuinely need from my college education?**
*(Consider: what would make this experience feel meaningful — not just successful)*
I need my college education to provide me w/ more than just a degree. I want to gain hands-on cybersecurity experience, strengthen my technical skills, build professional connections, & develop the knowledge necessary to be prepared for a successful career in cybersecurity. 


**3. What is the most important thing I learned about myself in Module 2?**
*(Consider: something surprising, clarifying, or that shifted how you see yourself)*
I have learned that persistence & curiosity are some of my strongest qualities but I also need to improve my time management it has been the most troubling. I realized that I am willing to keep learning & asking questions when I do not fully understand something, but procrastination can make things harder than they need to be.

**4. What kind of work energizes me? What drains me?**
*(Consider: from your Empathy Map and Ikigai reflection)*
Working involving cybersecurity, technology, problem solving, & learning new systems energizes me b/c I enjoy figuring out how things work/ function & finding solutions. Work drains me when it feels repetitive disconnected from my goals, or simply when I spend to much time overthinking details. 

**5. What problem or challenge do I most want to contribute to solving in the world?**
*(Consider: even a vague answer is useful here)*
I want to help protect people, organizations, & important systems from cyber threats. I’m especially interested in cybersecurity involving defense technology, emerging technology, & complex threats where strong security can prevent serious real-world consequences.

## Step 3 — Draft Your POV Statement

Use the following template to draft your POV Statement. Write it in your own voice — not in formal or academic language. Write **at least two drafts** of your POV Statement and choose the one that feels most honest and most useful.

> "I am [brief description of who you are — background, identity, situation]. I need [a genuine academic or career need you identified]. Because [the most important insight from your self-assessment work]."

**Example:**
> "I am a first-generation college student from a small town who has always been drawn to science but also deeply values connection to people and community. I need a degree path that lets me combine analytical thinking with direct human impact, because I have learned that I lose motivation and meaning when my work feels disconnected from real people's lives."

### Draft 1
I am an online college student pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Applied Cybersecurity & Minor in Artificial Intelligence & I am working toward a career in cybersecurity & defense technology/industry. I need a more centralized degree path that gives me strong technical knowledge, hands-on experience, & professional connections. Since taking these propelling steps I have learned that persistence & curiosity are major strengths for me, but I also need to improve my time management & gain more real-world experience.

### Draft 2
I am a cybersecurity student who enjoys technology, problem-solving, & learning how complex systems work. I need my education to prepare me for a career where I can protect important systems shoot amongst the stars in emerging technology, to continue my flow of learning, & work w/ advanced technology. B/c I have realized that I am most motivated when I am challenged, learning something new, & working toward a goal that has a real-world impact in propelling me to perform in the skies.

### Final POV Statement
*(Choose the draft — or a refined combination 

> I am an online college student pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Applied Cybersecurity w/ a strong interest in cybersecurity, defense technology, & emerging systems. I need my education to give me a strong foundation of technical skills, hand’s on experience, & professional connections that prepare me for a meaningful career. B/c I have learned that persistence, curiosity, & problem solving are some of my biggest strengths, & I am most motivated when I am able to apply those strengths to real world security challenges.

## Step 4 — Reflect

Below your final POV Statement, write a short paragraph (5–8 sentences) responding to the following:

- What was difficult about writing your POV Statement?
- What did you discover or clarify in the process?
- Does your POV Statement surprise you in any way?
- How does it connect to your Module 2 findings?

**My Reflection:**
Writing my POV Statement was difficult b/c I had to really narrow several interests & goals into one clear direction. The process helped me realize that cybersecurity isn’t just a some degree choice for me, but a field that connects strongly w/ the type of problems I enjoy solving. I also clarified that I want my education to give me hands on experience, technical growth, & professional connections instead of only helping me earn a degree. My POV statement didn’t completely surprise me, however it dial my career direction to feel more organized & specific. It really connects to my MOD 2 findings b/c my SWOT & Empathy Map showed that persistence, curiosity, & problem solving are strengths I can build on. They have also shown me that time management & gaining more real world cybersecurity experience are areas I still need to improve. Overall, the statement gives me a clearer starting point for deciding which opportunities & academic choice hoops fit my long term goals.
